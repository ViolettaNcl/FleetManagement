# Vehicle availability reservations

Reserve a vehicle in Maintenance for service, repair, inspection or other unavailability. Future start, positive duration up to 30 days, maximum 5,000 records. Half-open intervals allow adjacent assignments but reject overlap with registered trips or other reservations. Both manual and suggested dispatch honor reservations regardless of driver-shift mode. Calendar and assistant show scheduled blocks; vehicle deletion is protected. Cancel future reservations only. Active and elapsed records remain history. Elapsed is a time status, not a completed service record. Actual work is recorded separately in the maintenance journal. No automatic interval reset, no automatic reassignment, no server persistence or service-provider booking. Browser snapshot adds an optional field for compatibility.
